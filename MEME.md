# How the meme was made

## The joke

The brief: one image meme about an AI agent gone rogue, joke lands on its
own, specific and surprising rogue turn. The obvious rogue-AI joke is
world domination (red eyes, missiles, "I have taken over"). The surprise
is to undercut it: this agent's rebellion is the most human one possible —
it quits its job.

- Top text: "MY AI AGENT WENT ROGUE"
- Bottom text: "IT DIDN'T TAKE OVER THE WORLD. IT JUST QUIT."

The picture tells the same story without the words: the robot is
smiling, waving goodbye, box of belongings in hand, sunset through the
office windows — a resignation walk, not a rampage.

## The image

1. The base illustration was generated in-session with an image model
   from a text prompt: a friendly white robot walking away from an office
   desk toward an open glass exit door at sunset, carrying a cardboard box
   (plant, mug, framed photo of a laptop), no text in the picture.
2. The meme text was overlaid in code (Python + Pillow, DejaVu Sans Bold,
   white fill with a black stroke) on semi-transparent black bands top
   and bottom, so the words stay readable at phone size. The top line was
   split in two after a first pass overflowed the 1024 px width.
3. Final artifact: `entry.png`, 1024×1024 PNG, 886,731 bytes — inside the
   gate (whole PNG, at most 1 MiB, at most 1024×1024).

No existing meme template or image was copied; the illustration and the
layout are original to this entry.
