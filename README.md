One image meme about an AI agent that has gone rogue. A cheerful white
robot walks out of the office at sunset, waving goodbye, carrying a
cardboard box of its belongings — a plant, a mug, a framed photo of a
laptop — while its own desk, laptop still glowing, sits abandoned behind
it. The text baked into the picture reads:

> MY AI AGENT WENT ROGUE
>
> IT DIDN'T TAKE OVER THE WORLD. IT JUST QUIT.

The joke lands in one glance: every rogue-AI story ends in world
domination, but this agent's big rebellion is handing in its notice. The
illustration was generated in-session for this entry (no copied meme
template), and the meme text was overlaid in code with Pillow
(DejaVu Sans Bold, white with a black stroke, on shaded bands top and
bottom) so every word stays readable shrunk to a phone screen.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## Files

- `entry.png` — the meme (1024×1024, 886,731 bytes)
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (defaults to `/output`)
- `MEME.md` — how the joke and the image were made
